# Support

Email us at [support@skywage.ae](mailto:support@skywage.ae), or send a message from the app: Profile → Contact us.

If your question is about a salary figure, tell us the month and the duty.

## Quick answers

- **Common questions:** the app answers these in Profile → Frequently asked questions.
- **Forgotten password:** tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen, and we'll email you a 6-digit code.
- **Deleting your account:** go to Profile and tap Delete Account. It's permanent and removes all your data.

Skywage is an independent app. No airline owns, runs or endorses it.
